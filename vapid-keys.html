Make your notification keys

This page makes two keys that your app needs to send notifications. It works completely on this device — nothing is sent anywhere.

1. VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY

2. VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY — keep this secret

Never put this key in the app files, in a chat, or in an email. It goes only into Supabase (see SETUP.md).

Make the keys once. If you make new ones later, every phone has to turn notifications on again.
